# Content Pillars for Automated Social Publishing

## Pillar 1: lead_sourcing
- **Title**: Automated Lead Sourcing for Freelancers
- **Badge**: LEAD GEN AUTOMATION
- **Focus**: How manual lead scraping is a waste of time. The power of finding local businesses that desperately need a website without manually checking Google Maps.
- **Audience**: Freelance web developers, web design agency owners.

## Pillar 2: web_dev_struggles
- **Title**: The Reality of Freelance Web Development
- **Badge**: FREELANCE LIFE
- **Focus**: Spending 80% of time doing sales instead of coding. The frustration of bad clients, scope creep, and unpredictable income. Empathy for the freelancer grind.
- **Audience**: Upwork freelancers, WordPress developers, Shopify experts.

## Pillar 3: build_in_public
- **Title**: Building a SaaS in Public
- **Badge**: BUILD IN PUBLIC
- **Focus**: The transparent journey of building a Lead Sourcing Tool from scratch. Architecture decisions, marketing wins, and tech stack choices.
- **Audience**: Indie hackers, SaaS founders, early-stage operators.

## Pillar 4: client_acquisition
- **Title**: Modern Client Acquisition Strategies
- **Badge**: GROWTH STRATEGY
- **Focus**: Why sending generic emails doesn't work anymore. The value of hyper-targeted outreach (e.g., reaching out to a dentist with no website and offering a mockup).
- **Audience**: Freelance marketers, web devs, agency owners.

## Pillar 5: cold_outreach
- **Title**: Cold Email & Outreach Tactics
- **Badge**: B2B SALES
- **Focus**: How to pitch web development services effectively. Structuring irresistible offers for local businesses.
- **Audience**: Web design agencies, solopreneurs.
