# HANDLING PUSHBACK & OBJECTIONS

Since the product is in development, you might encounter specific questions or objections. Here is how to handle them:

## 1. "Is the tool live yet?" / "Where is the link?"
**Response:** Be honest. "Not live just yet! I'm currently building it in public and writing the core scraping engine. Looking for a few beta testers right now—mind if I DM you when the MVP is ready?"

## 2. "Lead gen tools already exist (like Apollo, D7)."
**Response:** "100% true, but most of them just give you a massive list of emails. I'm building this specifically for web devs—it explicitly filters out businesses that already have a good website, so you only get leads that actually *need* your specific service."

## 3. "Cold emailing local businesses doesn't work."
**Response:** Agree but pivot. "It's definitely getting harder if you send generic spam. But if you reach out to a business that literally doesn't have a website and show them a quick mockup, the conversion rate is completely different. That's the gap I'm trying to close."

## 4. "How much will it cost?"
**Response:** "Still figuring out the pricing model, but the beta will be completely free. I want to make sure it actually helps devs land clients before charging anything."
